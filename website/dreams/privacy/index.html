Privacy policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026.

This policy covers Dreams for Apple TV and this support website. It does not describe the separate macOS app or its agent integrations.

The Apple TV app

Dreams renders rain and a clock locally on your Apple TV. No account is required. The app does not transmit your settings or rendered content to the developer, and does not include advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs.

Rain and clock preferences are saved in the app’s local storage. You can restore defaults in settings or remove the app to remove its local data.

Apple services

Apple distributes the app and handles purchases under its own privacy policy. The developer does not receive your payment-card details. Apple may process store and diagnostic information according to your Apple settings and Apple’s policies.

Support requests

If you email support, your email address, message and any attachments are processed to answer your request. Do not send sensitive information. To request deletion of support correspondence held by the developer, contact the email below. Apple and email-provider records remain subject to their own policies and retention requirements.

This website

This site does not include analytics scripts, advertising, tracking cookies or remote fonts. The language choice is carried in the page URL, not saved in a cookie. The hosting provider may process standard request information such as IP address, browser information and access time to serve and protect the website.

Contact and changes

Developer: Egor Merkushev. Privacy questions: egor.merkushev@yandex.ru. If data practices change, this page will be updated.

Приложение для Apple TV

Dreams рисует дождь и часы локально на Apple TV. Учётная запись не нужна. Приложение не передаёт настройки или изображение разработчику и не содержит рекламы, трекинга или SDK сторонней аналитики.

Настройки дождя и часов хранятся локально в приложении. Их можно сбросить в настройках; удаление приложения удаляет его локальные данные.
